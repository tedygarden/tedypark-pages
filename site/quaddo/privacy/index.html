QuadDo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-01

QuadDo stores your task data on your device and in Apple-managed app group storage so the app and widget can stay in sync.

What data QuadDo handles

  • Task titles
  • Optional notes
  • Optional due dates
  • Completion state
  • Creation dates

How the data is used

  • To display and organize your tasks in the app
  • To show your tasks in widgets
  • To sync task state between the app and widget on the same device

Data sharing

QuadDo does not sell your data. QuadDo does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks. QuadDo does not send your task data to external servers.

Your choices

You can delete tasks at any time inside the app. Removing the app also removes locally stored app data according to iOS behavior.
